# UMAIR HUB

### Your Personal AI Study & Productivity Hub

UMAIR HUB is designed around one idea: **upload your study material once, then turn it into everything you need to learn, revise, practice, test, create, and present.**

## Product vision

- 📂 Material Library — PDF, PPT/PPTX, DOC/DOCX, images, scans, audio and web sources
- 🧠 AI Tutor — source-grounded explanations and guided learning
- 📝 Smart Notes — quick, detailed, exam and last-minute revision notes
- 🧪 Practice — MCQs, flashcards, adaptive questions and mistake book
- 📋 Exams — papers, mock tests, answer keys and exam simulator
- 🎨 Presentations — source-to-slide generation, speaker notes and exports
- 🎤 Viva & Presentation Practice — AI examiner and rehearsal
- 📅 Planning — study planner, calendar, deadlines and smart reminders
- 📊 Progress — mastery, weak areas, analytics and exam readiness
- 📑 Academic Work — assignment, research and citation assistance
- 🎧 Multimodal Learning — audio summaries, visual explanations and study packs
- 👥 Collaboration — sharing and group study features
- 👨‍🏫 Teacher Mode — assessments and teaching material generation

## Product principle

**ONE SOURCE → EVERYTHING**

Every generated answer should be grounded in the user's selected material whenever the task is source-based, with clear source/page references where available.

## Development approach

UMAIR HUB will be built as a modular, provider-agnostic application. AI providers can be swapped without rebuilding the product, allowing development to start with free/local options and add paid providers when needed.

## Roadmap

1. Product foundation & premium UI
2. Material Library and source ingestion
3. AI workspace and source-grounded chat
4. Notes, summaries and study packs
5. MCQ/quiz/test engine
6. Presentation generator and editor
7. Exams, mistakes and analytics
8. Viva, presentation practice and study planning
9. Multimodal features and teacher mode
10. Collaboration, optimization and production hardening
